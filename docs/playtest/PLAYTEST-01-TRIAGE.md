# PLAYTEST 01 — Triage

- P0: crash, corruption/loss of progress, impossible campaign, missing essential runtime assets, unavailable production, invalid gameplay authority.
- P1: broken AI/objective/essential interaction or severe responsive/visual regression that prevents or materially obscures gameplay.
- P2: polish, comfort, animation, minor readability or non-systemic balance feedback.

Every P0/P1 requires reproduction steps, device/viewport, expected result, observed result and evidence when available.
