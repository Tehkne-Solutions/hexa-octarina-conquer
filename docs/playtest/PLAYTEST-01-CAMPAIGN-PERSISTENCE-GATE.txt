HOC PLAYTEST 01 — CAMPAIGN PERSISTENCE GATE

Parent: #268

Goal:
Prove the certified RC campaign progress survives a fresh PostgreSQL campaign-store instance, equivalent to reopening/reloading the authoritative persistence layer.

Required behavior:
- complete c1-m1 and persist stars/progress;
- close the original PostgresCampaignStore;
- create a fresh PostgresCampaignStore against the same database;
- confirm c1-m1 completion, stars, totals and c1-m2 unlock survive;
- replay c1-m1 with a worse result through the fresh store;
- confirm the best star count does not regress while attempts advance.

This is a test-only playtest gate. No gameplay, campaign rule, UI, asset, balance or runtime-authority behavior is changed.
